# Ảnh demo tạo bằng ImageGen

Hai ảnh được tạo bằng công cụ ImageGen tích hợp, không dùng API key. Đây là ảnh tổng hợp để trình diễn, không phải ảnh quan sát học sinh.

## before.png — prompt

Use case: product-mockup. Asset type: calibration photograph for a school food-waste computer vision prototype. Create ONE realistic square top-down orthographic photograph of a matte dark charcoal rectangular cafeteria tray, centered and aligned exactly parallel to image borders on a plain pale gray table. The tray has exactly THREE equal-sized vertical rectangular compartments, side by side in a single row, with thick dark dividers. Left compartment: a compact oval mound of plain steamed white rice. Middle compartment: a compact oval portion of bright green cooked broccoli, no sauce. Right compartment: four clearly separated pieces of golden brown boneless chicken, no garnish or vegetables. Each food portion occupies about 65 percent of the inner area of its compartment; enough empty matte charcoal tray is visible around each food portion. Entire tray spans about x=8% to92%, y=20% to80% of square image. No bowls, no soup, no utensils, no people, no text, no watermark. Diffuse even overhead lighting, minimal shadows, crisp realistic food texture, no glossy reflections. Colors must clearly separate white rice, green broccoli, golden-brown chicken and dark charcoal tray. This is the BEFORE full-portion reference photograph. Save the generated project image and provide its local file path.

## after.png — prompt sửa ảnh before.png

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: the supplied top-down three-compartment cafeteria tray photo. Create the AFTER-meal version. Keep the exact same camera, square canvas, tray boundaries, three compartments, table, lighting and scale. Change ONLY the amount of food. Left: remove the upper half of the white rice mound, leaving about HALF its original visible area in the lower part of the left compartment, with a slightly irregular eaten edge. Middle: remove about THREE QUARTERS of the broccoli; leave only two or three small florets in the lower part of the middle compartment, about ONE QUARTER of its original visible area. Right: remove ALL four chicken pieces, leaving the right compartment empty and clean apart from one or two tiny crumbs. Preserve the dark matte tray texture where food was removed. No text, labels, numbers, utensils, hands, extra objects, or new food. This controlled after-meal photo is used to measure remaining food area relative to the original reference. Do not move or resize any tray part.
